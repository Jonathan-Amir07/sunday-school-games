    THE STORY REFERENCE

    Jonathan & David

    Follow these five moments. You can come back and look whenever you need help.

    1. They become friends. Jonathan loved David as he loved himself. 1 SAMUEL 18:1–3
    2. Jonathan shares his robe. He gives David his robe and other belongings as a sign of their bond. 1 SAMUEL 18:4
    3. Jonathan speaks up. When David is in danger, Jonathan speaks well of him to Saul and warns David. 1 SAMUEL 19:1–6
    4. The arrows carry a message. Jonathan uses an agreed signal in the field so David knows it is time to leave. 1 SAMUEL 20:35–42
    5. Jonathan brings courage. He visits David and strengthens his faith in God. 1 SAMUEL 23:16–18
